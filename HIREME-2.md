WORKING WITH ME: A SHORT GUIDE
==============================

I do my best work when goals are stated plainly and we talk regularly. This
page says what you can expect from me and what helps me deliver for you.


WHAT I BRING
------------

I like turning "impossible" into "done." That is the work I enjoy most and
the work I am best at.

  * Thirty years of experience working with many kinds of teams and
    managers.

  * Strong delivery on well-defined work. When the goal, the deadline, and
    the reason are clear, I tend to deliver early, under budget, and beyond
    spec.

  * Planning and analysis. I am most valuable when charting a clear path
    for a project, and when gathering and summarizing data from several
    points of view.

  * Care for craft. I want the work to be good, and I will tell you
    honestly when I think it is not there yet.


WHAT HELPS ME DO MY BEST WORK
-----------------------------

  * Tell me what you need done, by when, and why.
    With those three things clear at the start, I can move quickly and
    independently.

  * A 30-minute conversation every other week.
    It keeps us aligned as priorities shift. This one habit has made the
    biggest difference in my career.

  * Say it in words.
    I am better with explicit requests than with hints or tone. If it
    matters, please say it out loud.

  * Ask twice for my opinion on how we work.
    I hold back so I don't overstep. A second ask tells me you really want
    my view.

  * Protect time for important, non-urgent work.
    I see a lot of value in the work that prevents the next fire, not only
    in fighting today's.

  * Tell me early when I've missed the mark.
    I would much rather adjust in week one than find out at the end.


HOW I COMMUNICATE
-----------------

I am direct and honest, and I welcome the same from you.

I can run long, in writing and in speech. Brevity is a courtesy, and I am
working on it. If I am giving you more than you need, please interrupt me.
I will take it as help.

My natural order is to build up from the facts. If you want the
recommendation first, ask for the bottom line up front and you will get it.

I take feedback seriously, and sometimes more heavily than it was meant. If
you only intended a small comment, saying so helps me give it the right
weight.

If a conversation gets heated, I may ask to pause and pick it up later.
That is me making sure I come back with a clear head.


HOW I WORK
----------

My instinct is good, then simple, then quick. Many managers need quick,
then simple, then good. I can work your way. Tell me which matters most on
a given task and I will follow your lead.

I lean toward rules and established process. If you see a faster route,
point it out. I am happy to look at it with you and agree on when a
shortcut makes sense.

With colleagues, I share goals and leave the methods to them. I trust
people to know their own jobs.

Trust on a new team takes time to build. I expect that, and I am patient
with it.


IF SOMETHING SEEMS OFF
----------------------

  If you notice:  I am explaining at length.
  Please say:     "Give me the short version."

  If you notice:  I have delivered more than, or something different from,
                  what you wanted.
  Please say:     "Here is what I actually needed." Then we reset, no hard
                  feelings.

  If you notice:  I am quiet in a discussion where you want my input.
  Please say:     "I'd like your view on this." Ask a second time if I
                  hedge.

  If you notice:  I seem focused on the wrong priority.
  Please say:     "This is the urgent one, and here is why."

  If you notice:  I seem to be dwelling on a piece of feedback.
  Please say:     "That was a small point, not a big one."


WHAT KEEPS ME MOTIVATED
-----------------------

Three things keep me at my best: having fun, making a difference, and
learning. When a role offers all three, I stay and I deliver. I have been
in my current role since November 2015.


MY ASK
------

I am asking for a small investment: a clear statement of what you need, and
half an hour every other week. In return you get someone who wants to be
here, cares about the work, and will put everything into delivering it. Bet
on me, and I will make it worth your while.

Thank you for reading. If anything here is unclear, ask me. I would rather
talk it through than have either of us guess.
